Algorithm: Optimistic reliable broadcast
Propose. The broadcaster P_k sends ⟨propose, m⟩ to all parties.
Echo. Upon receiving the first ⟨propose, m⟩ message from the broadcaster, party P_i sends ⟨echo, m⟩ to all parties.
Vote. Upon receiving ⟨echo, m⟩ from ⌈n/2⌉ non-broadcaster parties, send ⟨vote, m⟩ to all parties if not already sent.
Ready. Party P_i sends ⟨ready, m⟩ to all parties (if not already sent) under either condition:  
  - Upon receiving ⟨echo, m⟩ from ⌈(n + f - 1)/2⌉ non-broadcaster parties,  
  - Upon receiving ⟨vote, m⟩ from ⌈(n + f - 1)/2⌉ non-broadcaster parties,  
  - Upon receiving f + 1 ⟨ready, m⟩ messages.
Opt Commit. Upon receiving ⟨echo, m⟩ from ⌈(n + 2f - 2)/2⌉ non-broadcaster parties, party P_i commits m and terminates.
{3,4}-step Commit. Upon receiving 2f + 1 ⟨ready, m⟩ messages, party P_i commits m and terminates.
